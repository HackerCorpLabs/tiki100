                About TIKI-100
                

                    
                

            

            

                
The TIKI-100 was a Norwegian CP/M-compatible computer launched in 1984 by Tiki Data.

                

                    CPU:Zilog Z80A @ 4MHz
                    RAM:64 KB + 32 KB VRAM
                    ROM:TIKI-ROM V2.03 W
                    Sound:AY-3-8912 PSG
                    FDC:FD1771
                    HDC:WD1010
                

                

                
Original emulator by Asbjorn Djupdal (2000-2001).

                Z80 CPU by Marat Fayzullin.

                AY-3-8912 sound from MAME (Couriersud).

                WD1010 HDC from RetroCore (Ronny Hansen).
